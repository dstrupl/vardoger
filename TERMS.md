## 2. What vardoger provides

Vardoger is a local developer tool that reads conversation history already
stored by a supported AI assistant and helps that assistant generate
personalization instructions. The project does not provide a hosted account,
cloud storage service, or paid subscription.

Model-side processing is performed by the AI assistant you choose to use.
